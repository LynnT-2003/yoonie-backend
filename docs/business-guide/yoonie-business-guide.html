Yoonie: How the Hotel Runs
A plain-language guide to the operating system and the AI concierge, for the business team
Part A · The Operating System

1 What Yoonie Is

Yoonie runs a hotel from the moment a guest books to the moment they leave. It takes bookings directly, works out what needs doing for each guest, sends that work to the right person's phone, and shows managers everything live: who is doing what, what is late, and what it costs.

Most hotels run on a booking system, a till, paper lists and group chats that don't talk to each other. Yoonie puts them in one place. A booking on the website instantly becomes a room to clean, a driver to send and a tour seat to fill, and every step of that work is visible and recorded.

1.1 What we promise the hotel

PromiseWhat it means day to day
The guest promise is keptThe room is ready when the guest walks in. The driver is at the arrivals door when the flight lands. The tour leaves on time.
Nothing happens blindlyEvery job has a person, a deadline and a next step. When something changes, the right people are told, and only them.
Everything is on recordEvery step is saved with who did it and when. That's what lets us measure, improve, and later let AI run parts of the hotel.

Everything in Yoonie follows the same loop:

3 Bookings

Guests book everything in one checkout on the hotel's own website: room, airport pickup, tours, spa and dining. Booking direct means the hotel keeps the 15–25% an online travel agent would take, and keeps the guest's details.

  • No double booking. While a guest checks out, their room and seats are held for 15 minutes. If they leave, everything is released automatically.
  • Pay at the hotel. Bookings are confirmed immediately and paid at the front desk. Online payment is planned.
  • Every booking remembers where it came from (Instagram, Google, a campaign), so we can show which marketing actually brings guests.
  • Missing flight details are flagged so the front desk can ask the guest before the pickup day.

4 Rooms Ready on Time

A room is only ready when a supervisor has inspected it. Housekeepers clean rooms in the order guests arrive, and everyone can see whether each room will make it.

flowchart LR
    A["Needs cleaning"] -->|Housekeeper taps Start| B["Being cleaned"]
    B -->|Housekeeper taps Done| C["Clean, waiting for inspection"]
    C -->|Supervisor passes| D["Ready for the guest"]
    C -->|Supervisor fails, says why| A
    D -->|Problem found, says why| A
QuestionAnswer
Who cleans which room?Rooms are open to any housekeeper; the first to tap Start takes it. Managers can also hand rooms out.
By when?One hour after the guest is expected: their flight landing time, or check-in time if they have no flight.
How do we know it's on time?Every room shows a countdown. It turns amber when time is tight and red when it's late.
What if the guest is early?When the driver picks the guest up, the housekeeper gets an urgent alert with the new arrival time.
What if inspection fails?The supervisor writes what's wrong; the same housekeeper gets it straight away.

7 Money at the Front Desk

Every guest has a clear bill: rooms, extras, 10% service charge and 7% VAT, what's been paid and what's still owed.

  • The front desk records each payment: cash, card, PromptPay or bank transfer, with the slip number.
  • Deposits are fine; overpaying isn't possible.
  • Every payment shows who took it and when.

10 The Rules We Never Break

These rules apply to every kind of job, for people today and robots tomorrow. A feature that breaks one is a bug, even if it seems to work.

#RuleIn practice
1The person doing the work reports itOnly the housekeeper, driver or guide marks their step. The desk can record it for them if they call in, and it says so.
2One fact, one placeA room's status is changed one way only, so screens never disagree.
3Every job is accountableWho has it, by when, what's next, where it is, and what happens if it goes wrong.
4Tell the right people, only themNo noise, no silence.
5Problems must be answeredStaff raise problems from a short list; the desk must reply.
6Steps only move forwardSafe even with bad signal: a step sent twice counts once.
7Choices, not free textStatuses and reasons come from fixed lists so they can be counted; notes are extra.
8People decide anything that costs money or affects a guestCancelling a tour, a no-show, refunds, free upgrades: a person decides. AI can suggest, not decide.
9Same rules for people and robotsA robot gets jobs and reports steps exactly like a person.
10Each hotel only sees its own dataStaff only see and act on their own branch.
Part B · The AI Concierge

11 The Vision: A Full AI Concierge

A concierge that never sleeps, speaks the guest's language, and is connected to the real hotel: it can see live availability, the guest's booking and the state of their room, and it can create real jobs for staff. The same AI helps staff run the day, and gives owners a plain-English view of the business.

The guest concierge and the staff assistant are two separate assistants that never share a conversation. The guest one can only read public information and the guest's own booking, and can only ask for things. The staff one acts with exactly the permissions of the signed-in staff member.

13 How We Keep It Safe

The concierge is built so that a guest can never act like staff, and staff can never do more through chat than in the app.

SafeguardHow it works
Two separate assistantsGuests and staff use different assistants with different doors. They never share a conversation or a memory.
Staff must sign inThe staff assistant only answers someone signed in to Yoonie, and acts with that person's own permissions.
A booking is privateA guest sees a booking only with its booking number and the email or phone on it. A wrong detail gets the same polite "not found".
Live data onlyPrices, availability and bookings are read from Yoonie at the moment of the question, never from a script.
People decide moneyLate checkout, upgrades, discounts, refunds and cancellations are passed to a manager; the AI never approves them.
Everything is recordedEvery request and action is saved with who and when, like any other action in Yoonie.
